# Nexus Retail — Marketing ROI & Profitability Analysis

## Overview

An Excel-based analysis of Nexus Retail's digital marketing investment to determine which platforms, strategies, products, and markets are generating profitable returns.

## Business Question

> Is our marketing investment actually profitable, and where should management increase, reduce, or reallocate spending?

## Tools

- Microsoft Excel
- Power Query
- PivotTables & PivotCharts
- Excel Dashboard

## Analysis

The project evaluates:

- Platform performance
- Campaign strategy
- Customer segments
- Product categories
- Regional performance
- Monthly performance

## Key Findings

- **LinkedIn Ads:** 0.97× ROAS and approximately **−₹3.49L marketing profit**
- **TikTok:** highest ROAS at **5.97×** and approximately **₹6.50L profit**
- **Meta:** approximately **₹2.79L profit** with **16.25% margin**
- **Retargeting:** strongest strategy at **6.80× ROAS** and approximately **₹4.99L profit**
- **Shopping:** approximately **−₹18K marketing profit**
- **Electronics:** high revenue but loss-making due to high product costs

## Recommendation

Reallocate investment away from LinkedIn and underperforming campaigns toward stronger areas, particularly **Retargeting, Meta and TikTok**.

A **70% Meta / 30% TikTok** allocation is proposed as a starting scenario for reallocated funds, with future allocation adjusted based on actual profitability.
